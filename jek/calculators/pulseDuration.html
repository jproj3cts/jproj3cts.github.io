Mode-locked pulse duration

The values filled in are an example: a Ti:sapphire oscillator with a 10 nm wide spectrum at 800 nm.

Input parameters
Δν = Spectral bandwidth (FWHM) in frequency; a bandwidth in wavelength is converted with Δν = cΔλ/λ², which holds while Δλ is much smaller than λ
K = Time-bandwidth product of a transform-limited pulse: 2 ln 2/π ≈ 0.441 for a Gaussian, 0.315 for sech²
$$\tau_\mathrm{FWHM} = \frac{K}{\Delta \nu}$$

Output parameter
τFWHM = Shortest pulse duration the spectrum allows (transform limited, no chirp). A chirped pulse is longer.
