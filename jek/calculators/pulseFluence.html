Pulsed laser fluence

The values filled in are an example: a Q-switched Nd:YAG laser, 100 mJ pulses in a beam of 3 mm radius.

Input parameters
Epulse = Pulse energy
w = Gaussian beam radius where the intensity falls to 1/e² (13.5%) of its peak; half the 1/e² diameter
$$F_0 = \frac{2E_\mathrm{pulse}}{\pi w^2} \qquad \bar F = \frac{E_\mathrm{pulse}}{\pi w^2}$$
Peak fluence F₀ = —
Average fluence F̄ = —
Output parameters
F0 = Fluence on the axis of a Gaussian beam, the figure to compare with a damage threshold
F̄ = Pulse energy divided by the area πw², half the peak; 1 J/cm² = 10⁴ J/m²

Formula: B. E. A. Saleh and M. C. Teich, Fundamentals of Photonics, 3rd ed. (Wiley, 2019).
