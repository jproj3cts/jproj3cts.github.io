Privacy Policy

Version 1.2, 5 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data JEK Systems collects through jeksys.net, the JEKray2D optical bench simulator and JEKray2D Pro accounts, why, and what you can do about it. JEK Systems is the controller of this data under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Contact us about anything here at support@jeksys.net.

1. The short version

  • You can use JEKray2D without an account. Your benches then stay on your own device and we never receive them.
  • We use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.
  • If you take out JEKray2D Pro, we keep what is needed to run your account: your name, your email address if your sign-in provider gives it, your benches, and the state of your subscription. Card details stay with Stripe; we never see them.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • You can export everything and delete your account at any time.

2. Using the website and JEKray2D without an account

JEKray2D runs entirely in your browser. Benches you build, open or save stay on your device: in your browser’s own storage (for autosave and preferences such as which parts palette you use) and in any .jekray files you save. None of this is sent to us.

Share links carry the bench inside the part of the link after the #. Browsers do not send that part to the website, so we do not receive the bench; whoever you give the link to does.

Bug reports open an email in your own email program, addressed to us, containing your description, your browser and screen details, the bench’s name and size, and a link to the bench. Nothing is sent until you send that email. We use it only to fix the problem and to reply to you.

Hosting. jeksys.net is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records technical details of each visit, such as your IP address and browser, to deliver and secure the site; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Some calculator pages load a maths typesetting library from the jsDelivr network, which sees the same kind of technical details.

Shop. If you buy something on jeksys.net, payment is handled by Stripe, and we receive your name, email address, billing country and what you bought, to deliver it and keep tax records. Delivery emails are sent through Resend. The quote cart keeps its contents in your browser’s storage until you send us a quote request.

Emails to us. If you email us, we keep the conversation to answer you and for our records.

3. JEKray2D Pro accounts

JEKray2D Pro is the paid subscription that adds cloud storage, version history and team workspaces to JEKray2D. When you have an account we hold:

  • Sign-in details. You sign in with Google, Microsoft or ORCID; we never see or store a password. From Google we receive your Google account identifier, your name and your verified email address. From Microsoft (university and work accounts only) we receive your account’s and your organisation’s identifiers, your name and your sign-in name, which is usually your work email address. From ORCID we receive your ORCID iD and name, and your email address only if you have made it public. We use these only to sign you in and to show your name to people you work with.
  • Sessions. When you sign in we set one cookie (see section 6) and keep a scrambled copy of it with the time it was created, when it expires and your browser’s description, so you can see and end your sessions.
  • Your benches. The benches, folders and earlier versions you save to the cloud, with who saved each version and when.
  • Teams. The workspaces you belong to and your role in each. If someone invites you, we hold the email address or ORCID iD they invited until you accept or the invitation expires.
  • Subscription. Your plan, number of seats, status and renewal date, and Stripe’s reference numbers for you. For the academic price, we record how you qualified: your university email domain or your ORCID employment or education record.
  • University licences. If your university has a JEKray2D Pro licence for its people, we record that it covers you and how (your university email domain, your university’s Microsoft account, an invitation from its licence administrators, or a current affiliation with it on your ORCID record), and the months in which you used JEKray2D Pro through it. We tell the university only how many people used it each month. Its licence administrators can invite people and take people off by email address, but we never tell them whether a particular person uses JEKray2D.
  • Activity records. A log of significant actions in shared workspaces, such as who deleted or restored a bench, so that team owners can see what happened.

Google account data. We request only the basic scopes needed to sign you in (openid, email and profile) and access no other Google data. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To provide JEKray2D Pro, as our contract with you: signing you in, storing and showing your benches, running teams and taking payment.
  • To keep the service secure and working, as our legitimate interest: preventing abuse, investigating faults and keeping activity records for teams.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping tax and accounting records.
  • To tell you about your account: receipts, payment problems, invitations and important changes to the service. We send marketing email only if you ask for it, and you can stop it at any time.

5. Who else handles it

We use these providers, each under a data processing agreement or acting under its own privacy policy as shown:

  • Cloudflare runs the JEKray2D Pro service and stores account data and benches.
  • Stripe takes payments, holds card details and produces invoices. Stripe is also an independent controller for some data, such as fraud checks; see Stripe’s privacy policy.
  • Resend sends account emails such as invitations and receipts.
  • Google, Microsoft and ORCID confirm who you are when you sign in with them, under their own privacy policies.
  • GitHub hosts the website.

Members of a team workspace see each other’s names, roles and the benches in that workspace. We may disclose data if the law requires it. We never sell personal data.

Transfers outside the UK. Some of these providers process data in the United States and elsewhere. Where they do, the transfer is protected by UK adequacy regulations (including the UK extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

6. Cookies and browser storage

JEKray2D Pro sets one cookie, jek_session, on api.jeksys.net when you sign in. It keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, cannot be read by web pages, and is removed when you sign out. It is strictly necessary for the service you asked for, so it needs no consent banner.

JEKray2D and the shop also keep preferences, autosaves and your quote cart in your browser’s storage on your device. We cannot read this storage; clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

We set no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account data and benches: while you have an account. A bench you delete stays in the bin for 30 days so you can restore it, then it and its history are deleted.
  • When you delete your account: your personal workspace and benches are deleted at once, and remaining copies in our providers’ backups are overwritten within 30 days. Benches you saved in a team workspace stay with that team.
  • After a subscription ends: see the JEKray2D Pro Terms for how long your cloud benches stay available to open and export.
  • Sessions: deleted when you sign out or when they expire.
  • Invoices and payment records: six years, as UK tax law requires.
  • University licence usage records: for the life of the licence and six years afterwards, as the basis of what the university was invoiced. When you delete your account they are no longer linked to your name or email address.
  • Emails with us: up to three years after our last contact.

8. Your rights

You have the right to see the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to our use of it, and to receive it in a portable form. In JEKray2D Pro you can download everything we hold about you and delete your account from the account menu in JEKray2D; for anything else, email support@jeksys.net. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Security

All connections are encrypted. Sessions are stored only as one-way hashes, every request is checked against the workspaces you belong to, and access to our systems is limited to JEK Systems. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach puts your data at risk we will tell you and the Information Commissioner’s Office as the law requires.

10. Children

JEKray2D can be used by anyone without an account. JEKray2D Pro accounts are for people aged 13 and over, and subscriptions must be bought by an adult.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when what we collect or how we use it changes. The version and date at the top will change, and if the change is significant we will tell account holders by email before it takes effect.
