Cavity stability and g-factors

The values filled in are an example: a 100 mm cavity with two 500 mm concave mirrors.

Input parameters:
L = distance between the mirrors
R₁, R₂ = mirror radii of curvature: positive for a concave mirror (facing into the cavity), negative for a convex one, 0 for a flat mirror
$$ g_1 = 1 - \frac{L}{R_1}, \quad g_2 = 1 - \frac{L}{R_2} $$
$$ 0 \le g_1 g_2 \le 1 $$

Output parameters:
g₁, g₂ = cavity g-factors
Stability = stable if 0 < g₁g₂ < 1; at g₁g₂ = 0 or 1 the cavity is on the edge of stability (for example confocal, concentric or flat–flat)

Formula: A. E. Siegman, Lasers (University Science Books, 1986).
