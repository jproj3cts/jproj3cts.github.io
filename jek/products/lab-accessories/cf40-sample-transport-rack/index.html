CF40 Vacuum-Compatible Hydrophobic Sample Transport Rack - 16 (30 x 30 x 1 mm)

£45.00 + VAT

Part number JEK-CF40-SR16

Description

A rack that holds up to 16 substrates, 30 × 30 × 1 mm, and slides into a standard CF40 vacuum transport tube, so a batch of samples can move between deposition, a glovebox, storage and characterisation without being handled one at a time.

It is 3D printed in black polypropylene. Polypropylene is hydrophobic, takes up very little moisture and resists most laboratory solvents. The rack is reusable: clean it with any procedure that suits polypropylene and let it dry fully before it goes under vacuum.

It is made for transport and storage under vacuum, not as a permanent part of a UHV chamber, and it should not be baked out. How well it behaves under vacuum depends on how it is cleaned and pumped; the vacuum compatibility statement below has the details.

Specifications

  • Holds 16 substrates of 30 × 30 × 1 mm
  • Fits a standard CF40 vacuum transport tube
  • Hydrophobic polypropylene, black, 3D printed
  • Reusable
  • For vacuum transport and storage; not for bakeout or permanent UHV use

Used for

  • Physical vapour deposition (PVD)
  • Perovskite and other thin-film research
  • Semiconductor and optical coating development
  • Moving samples between a glovebox and a vacuum chamber
