The whole experiment, light to oscilloscope

Lasers, lenses, fibre, photodiodes, lock-ins and much more, solved together on one bench. Wire a photodiode to an oscilloscope and see the trace your optical system would produce.

Validated against published papers

JEKray’s tight focus matches Richards and Wolf’s vector theory within 1% up to NA 0.8, and its resonator modes match Kogelnik and Li’s within 0.1%. Every lens, diffraction and resonator result on the validation page falls within a tolerance fixed before its first run.

See the validation page

Design and optimise your custom lens

Optimise your own lens in the Optical designer, check its yield with Monte Carlo tolerancing, then order it made through us (coming soon). Bring in your Zemax files, and send the workshop an ISO 10110 drawing and STEP solids.

How lens design works

Rays and the diffracted field

Rays keep the bench live while you drag parts. Switch on the diffracted field and it stays optimised, picking the right method for each stretch of beam so a tight focus and a long beam both run in a browser tab.

Pricing

The simulator is free, with or without an account, under the JEKray Licence. JEKrayPro adds the cloud and the designers’ design work. Prices include VAT where it applies; payment is taken by Stripe and you can cancel any time. If a subscription ends, every bench can still be opened and exported.
