The Module

Echo Station is a polar research base sunk over a fresh impact crater, built in secret by the High Commission to study the meteorite buried beneath the ice. Weeks ago, it stopped answering.

Restore communications. Account for the staff. Recover anything interesting.

Eight people wintered here. Three of them are already dead, and the survivors have a story that doesn't match the blood on the floor. Cold, close, and built on not knowing who to trust. Horror in the vein of The Thing.

What's Inside

  • Three keyed locations: the Boathouse, Base Camp and the Crater Summit. Plus an overview map of the site
  • Room plans for a barracks, dog shed, washroom, basement, lift platform, lab and medical unit
  • A minute-by-minute timeline of the morning it went wrong, so the station plays as an unfolding scene rather than a set of empty rooms
  • Eight named personnel, and what's left of them
  • A statblock for the thing out on the ice
  • A sunken ship, a frozen basement, and a dog team loose in the snow

You'll Need
