Parameters:
f = focal length
u = object distance
v = image distance, in the unit of u
M = lateral magnification
hₒ = object height
hᵢ = image height, in the unit of hₒ
Sign convention: real is positive. u is positive for a real object, v for a real image on the far side of the lens, f for a converging lens. A negative M or hᵢ means the image is inverted.

Formula: the Gaussian thin-lens equation and lateral magnification, E. Hecht, Optics, 5th ed. (Pearson, 2017).
