**JEKsuite** ([jeksys.net/jek/tools/jekspace.html](/jek/tools/jekspace.html)) is where the JEK apps and your cloud benches meet. It opens in a tab of its own: from an app, the cloud button beside the bench's name, **File › My benches on JEKsuite…**, or a link ending `#benches`. The same tab is reused each time, and so is each app's, so a bench chosen in JEKsuite opens in its app's tab rather than a new one.

## The apps

**New** in the bar starts a bench, opened in its app's own tab. Each app also has a page here, from the top of the left-hand list (see below). Signed out, the apps are under the sign-in panel. A new bench is a JEKray bench, an optical, RF or coating design (JEKray opens straight into that designer), or a JEKion bench. With JEKrayPro, or in a team with a plan, the new bench is made in the cloud first, in the workspace and folder you are looking at (or another you choose), and opens in its app already saving there. Without, it starts in the app itself, kept in that browser, where **Save to cloud** can put it in the cloud later.

## Your benches

Down the left: the apps, your workspaces (your own, and each team you are in), **All benches**, **Recent**, **My parts** (or a team's parts), **My links** and the **Bin**. Your folders are in the page, under **Folders**, and in the path along the top. Each bench is a card with its picture and a label for its app. Click a card to open the bench in its app. Its **⋯** menu gives:

- **History**: its versions, newest first, with the forks made from it drawn as branches. Open a copy of any version, **Compare** two versions item by item, **Fork from here**, or **Restore** one. A fork's history has **Merge this fork into it** and **Bring its changes in**: the merge is worked out item by item, any clash chosen by hand, and saved as a new version of the bench it goes into, the version before kept to come back to.
- **Fork**, **Copy to** another workspace, **Rename**, **Move to** a folder, and **Delete** (to the Bin for 30 days, where **Restore** brings it back).

**My parts** lists the parts and assemblies you kept from JEKray, to rename or delete; they are added to a bench from JEKray's **+ Add part › ★ My parts**. **My links** lists the short share links you made, how often each was opened, and lets you copy or delete one.


## An app's page

Click **JEKray** or **JEKion** at the top of the left-hand list for that app's page in JEKsuite, rather than the app itself; **Open JEKray** (or JEKion) there opens the app. The page says what the app does and has its buttons: **Open JEKray** (or JEKion), **New bench**, and for JEKray **Optical design**, **RF design** and **Coating design**. Under them are that app's benches alone, with the same folders, search, sorting and cards or rows as My benches; each folder says how many of the app's benches it holds. Beside them, the bench you picked is shown larger, with where it is, when it last changed and by whom, its size and its version: **Open in JEKray** opens it, **History** shows its versions, and **⋯** has the rest. Click a bench to pick it; double-click it, or press Enter, to open it. Until you pick one, the one changed last is shown.

Each app's page has its own address, `jekspace.html#app=jekray` or `#app=jekion`, so it can be bookmarked, and the browser's Back button comes back to it. Anything else in the left-hand list leaves it. Signed out, the page still says what the app does and opens it; its benches show once you sign in.

## Your account, plan and teams

The button at the top right signs you in (Google, Microsoft, or an email address with a link, a code or a password) and holds the account menu: your plan, **Your account** (your name, picture and password), **Billing and invoices**, switching between monthly and yearly billing, each of your **teams** (members, invitations, roles, the team's plan and billing, and the web domain it signs part packs with) and **Create a team**, a university's licence for those who look after it, linking another way to sign in, signing out, deleting the account, and the theme. One account signs you in to every app. **Get Pro** shows JEKrayPro's plans and prices.
