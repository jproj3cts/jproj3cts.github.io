Coherence length

The values filled in are an example: a helium-neon laser at 632.8 nm running on several modes across its 1.5 GHz Doppler-broadened gain line.

Input parameters
λ = Centre wavelength in vacuum (needed for a linewidth in nm or pm)
Δλ, Δν = Full width at half maximum of the spectrum, in wavelength or frequency (Δν = cΔλ/λ²)
a = Line-shape factor: √(2 ln 2/π) ≈ 0.664 for a Gaussian line, 1/π ≈ 0.318 for a Lorentzian, 1 for the rough estimate λ²/Δλ
$$ L_c = a\,\frac{c}{\Delta \nu} = a\,\frac{\lambda^2}{\Delta \lambda} \qquad \tau_c = \frac{L_c}{c} $$

Output parameters
Lc = Coherence length in vacuum, with the coherence time defined as τc = ∫|g(τ)|² dτ; in a medium divide by its group index
τc = Coherence time
