Import a material

  1. Find the material on refractiveindex.info and choose a data set that covers your wavelengths.
  2. Under the graph, use Data [CSV - nk] (or [CSV - n]) to download the file, or select the numbers and copy them.
  3. Drop the file below or paste the numbers. Wavelengths are in micrometres, which is what that site exports.

Tolerance study

Perturbs the swept parameters at random and traces the bench many times, to see how much the result moves. Each sweep’s From–To range is its tolerance range.

Optimise one value

Searches a swept parameter over its From–To range for the setting that gives the best result. Give the value a sweep first; its range is the search range.

What this models, and what it does not

Modelled. Exact tracing of conic and aspheric surfaces with real glass dispersion; reflection, refraction and total internal reflection; Fresnel coefficients and thin-film coatings with phase; polarisation as complex s and p amplitudes, with waveplates and polarisers; optical path, so wavefront error, interference and cavity round trips; Gaussian beam propagation along the axis; and diffraction at a screen, giving the point spread function, Strehl ratio and MTF.

Not modelled. Anything outside the plane: no skew rays, no out-of-plane tilts, no field analysis in two dimensions, no anamorphic systems. Also no scatter, no thermal effects, no coating dispersionn. Power figures ignore absorption unless you set it on a material.

How far to trust it. For anything that gets built, funded or published, reproduce the number in an established package first.

Import from a Zemax file

  1. Open the Thorlabs product page for the lens, mirror, beam splitter or prism and find its part number in the table.
  2. Click the document icon next to the part number and download the Zemax file (.zmx). If it arrives as a .zip, extract it first.
  3. Choose the file below, or drop it straight onto the bench.

The part is placed at the centre of the view with light travelling left to right, as in the Zemax file. Lenses and mirrors come across with their exact surfaces. Zemax describes cubes and prisms with tilted, folded surfaces that can't be read back reliably, so for those the importer takes the glass and size and builds the part itself. Pick the type above if it guesses wrong.

Glass the tracer doesn't recognise uses the nd and Vd values from the file, which is less accurate far from the visible.
