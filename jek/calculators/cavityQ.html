Cavity Q-factor and photon lifetime

The values filled in are an example: a cavity resonant at 1064 nm (281.8 THz) with a 4.8 MHz linewidth.

Input parameters:
ν₀ = resonance frequency
Δν = full width at half maximum of the resonance
$$ Q = \frac{\nu_0}{\Delta \nu} $$
Q = —
$$ \tau = \frac{Q}{2\pi \nu_0} = \frac{1}{2\pi \Delta\nu} $$
τ = —
Output parameters:
Q = quality factor of the resonance
τ = photon lifetime, the time for the stored energy to fall to 1/e

Formula: B. E. A. Saleh and M. C. Teich, Fundamentals of Photonics, 3rd ed. (Wiley, 2019).
