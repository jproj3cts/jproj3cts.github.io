Ways to work together

Design review

A short engagement on a design you already have. Schematics, optical layouts or a noise budget are looked over and you get back a written assessment: what will limit performance, what is likely to cause trouble in build or test, and what to change first.

Subsystem design

A defined block, a detector front end, a control loop, a mount, taken from requirements through to tested hardware, with the design files and test data handed over so you can build more of them yourself.

Complete instrument

Specification to working hardware. Optics, electronics, firmware and mechanics designed as one system, delivered with documentation and a characterisation report against the agreed requirements.
