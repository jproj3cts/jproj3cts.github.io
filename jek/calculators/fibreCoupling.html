Fibre coupling efficiency

The values filled in are an example: a 5 µm waist focused onto SMF-28 fibre at 1550 nm (mode field diameter 10.4 µm), 1 µm off axis.

Input parameters
w0 = beam waist radius (1/e² intensity), at the fibre face
wf = fibre mode field radius, half the mode field diameter on the datasheet
r = lateral offset between the beam axis and the fibre axis
$$\eta = \left(\frac{2 w_0 w_f}{w_0^2 + w_f^2}\right)^2 \exp\left(-\frac{2 r^2}{w_0^2 + w_f^2}\right)$$

Output parameters
η = fraction of the beam's power coupled into the fibre's fundamental mode
Loss = −10 log₁₀ η, in dB
This assumes Gaussian beam and mode, the beam waist at the fibre face, no tilt and no reflection loss.

Formula: overlap of two Gaussian modes with an offset, as in A. Ghatak and K. Thyagarajan, An Introduction to Fiber Optics (Cambridge University Press, 1998).
