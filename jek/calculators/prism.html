Prism deviation

The values filled in are an example: a 60° N-BK7 prism (n = 1.5168 at 587.6 nm) in air, with light arriving at 50°, close to minimum deviation.

Input parameters:
n = refractive index of the prism (relative to the surrounding medium)
A = apex angle
i = angle of incidence on the first face, from the normal. Positive angles are on the side of the normal away from the apex.
$$ r_1 = \arcsin\left(\frac{\sin i}{n}\right) $$
r₁ = —
$$ r_2 = A - r_1 $$
r₂ = —
$$ e = \arcsin(n \sin r_2) $$
e = —
$$ \delta = i + e - A $$
δ = —
$$ \delta_\text{min} = 2\arcsin\left(n \sin\frac{A}{2}\right) - A $$
δ_min = —
Output parameters:
r₁ = refraction angle inside the prism at the first face
r₂ = angle of incidence inside the prism at the second face
e = angle of emergence from the second face
δ = total deviation of the ray
δmin = minimum deviation, reached when the ray passes through symmetrically
Angles are given in degrees.

Formula: deviation by a dispersing prism, E. Hecht, Optics, 5th ed. (Pearson, 2017).
