JEKray2D, for teaching and research

JEKray2D is an optical bench simulator that runs in the browser. Students and researchers place sources, lenses, mirrors, fibres and other parts on a virtual breadboard and see the light traced through them as they move them.

  • Exact rays through real glass: spherical, conic and aspheric surfaces, real glass dispersion, Fresnel losses and coatings; spot size, wavefront error, MTF and Seidel aberrations.
  • Waves as well as rays: Gaussian beams through every branch of a system, and the diffracted field propagated surface by surface, so interferometers, double slits and standing waves behave as they should.
  • Cavities, polarisation, fibres and signals: resonators found and analysed automatically, fibre networks, detectors and the electronics between them.
  • Optical traps: trap depth and frequencies for atoms and dielectric particles, in vacuum, gas or water.

JEKray2D itself is free for everyone. It is used for lab design, for teaching optics, and for setting exercises that students can open with one link.

What JEKray2D Pro adds

  • Export of everything at any time, in the open .jekray format.

When every place is taken, someone new joins once a place frees: anyone who has not used it for a year frees theirs, or you can ask us to remove someone. You can move to a larger tier at any time.

  • Nothing is lost when a licence ends. People keep their accounts and can open and export all their benches.
