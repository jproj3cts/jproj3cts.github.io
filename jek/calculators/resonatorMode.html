Free spectral range

The values filled in are an example: a 100 mm linear cavity in air.

Input parameters:
n = refractive index of the medium filling the resonator (use the group index if it is dispersive)
L = mirror spacing for a linear cavity; the full round-trip length (circumference) for a ring
$$ \mathrm{FSR} = \frac{c}{2 n L} $$
$$ \mathrm{FSR} = \frac{c}{n L} $$
FSR = —
Round-trip time = —
Output parameters:
FSR = free spectral range, the frequency spacing between adjacent longitudinal modes
Round-trip time = 1/FSR, the time light takes to go once round the resonator

Formula: B. E. A. Saleh and M. C. Teich, Fundamentals of Photonics, 3rd ed. (Wiley, 2019).
