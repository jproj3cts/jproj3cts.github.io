The Module

Breitenwil is a town high in the Swiss Alps, a long way from anywhere. Above it sits the Echo Array: nine radio dishes that have spent the last month listening to something they should not.

You are a travelling mystery gang, hired by two worried parents to find their daughter. She is a radio astronomer stationed at the Array, and her letters home stopped a month ago.

What you find is a town that is quietly disappearing, and a population grieving for people they can no longer name.

Inspired by the False Hydra, the homebrew monster the tabletop community has been building on for over a decade.

What's Inside

  • Seven keyed locations, plus a map of the town
  • A cast of townsfolk being eaten one at a time, with a nightly feeding order that keeps the pressure on while the party investigate
  • A statblock for the Singer, adjustable for party size and level
  • A two-handed finale that splits the party across a mountainside and gives both halves something to do
  • Eleven omens to drop in as you go

Content Warning

Contains depictions of suicide, gore, violent death, grief, and memory loss. Clear this with your players before you begin.

You'll Need

The μRPG Basic Rules (free), a character sheet per player, and dice.
