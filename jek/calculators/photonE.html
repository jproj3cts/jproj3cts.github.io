Photon energy and wavelength

The values filled in are an example: a 1550 nm photon from the telecom C-band. Type in either box and the other one follows.

Input parameters
E = photon energy
λ = wavelength in vacuum
$$E = \frac{hc}{\lambda}$$
E = —
$$\lambda = \frac{hc}{E}$$
λ = —
$$\nu = \frac{c}{\lambda} = \frac{E}{h}$$
ν = —
Output parameters
E = photon energy, in joules and electronvolts
λ = wavelength in vacuum
ν = frequency
h = 6.62607015 × 10⁻³⁴ J s, c = 299 792 458 m/s, 1 eV = 1.602176634 × 10⁻¹⁹ J (exact SI values)

Formula: B. E. A. Saleh and M. C. Teich, Fundamentals of Photonics, 3rd ed. (Wiley, 2019).
