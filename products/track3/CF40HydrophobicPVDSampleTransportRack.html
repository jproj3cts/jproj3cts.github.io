CF40 Vacuum-Compatible Hydrophobic Sample Transport Rack - 16 (30 x 30 x 1 mm)

£249.00 + VAT

Part number JEK-CF40-SR16

Description

A compact, reusable sample carrier designed for the handling, transport, and storage of thin-film deposition substrates in vacuum environments.

The holder accommodates up to 16 individual 30 mm × 30 mm × 2 mm substrates, providing a high-density solution for perovskite and other thin-film research workflows. Designed to fit within standard CF40 vacuum tubing, the carrier enables protected transfer of multiple samples between deposition systems, gloveboxes, vacuum storage vessels, and characterisation equipment.

Manufactured from high-purity polypropylene (PP), the holder provides excellent chemical resistance and a naturally low surface energy, helping minimise contamination and unwanted interaction with sensitive surfaces.

Key Features

  • Holds 16 × 3 cm × 3 cm × 2 mm substrates
  • Compatible with CF40 vacuum transport systems
  • Designed for vacuum-compatible sample handling
  • Hydrophobic polypropylene construction
  • Low contamination risk for sensitive thin films
  • Enables batch processing and transport of multiple samples
  • Reusable laboratory accessory
  • Suitable for perovskite, semiconductor, optical coating, and thin-film research applications

Typical Applications

  • Perovskite photovoltaic research
  • Thin-film semiconductor deposition
  • Physical vapour deposition (PVD)
  • Vacuum sample transfer
  • Glovebox-to-chamber sample handling
  • Optical coating development
