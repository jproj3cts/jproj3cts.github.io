JEK Systems

Custom Solutions

Not every measurement has an off-the-shelf answer. Alongside the catalogue, JEK Systems takes on consultancy and contract design work in optical, electronic and mechanical engineering — from a second opinion on an existing design through to an instrument specified, built and tested from scratch.

The three disciplines are handled in-house and designed together, which matters most when the performance you need comes from how the optics, electronics and mechanics interact rather than from any one of them alone.

Capabilities

Optical

  • Photodetectors and low-noise front ends
  • Optical sensing and measurement systems
  • Laser and LED sources, drive and stabilisation
  • Free-space and fibre-coupled beam paths
  • Alignment, characterisation and noise budgets

Electronic

  • Low-noise analogue signal chains
  • Precision measurement and control electronics
  • Embedded firmware and instrument interfaces
  • FPGA design for real-time signal processing
  • Schematic capture, PCB layout and bring-up

Mechanical

  • Optomechanical mounts and assemblies
  • Drift- and vibration-sensitive structures
  • Vacuum-compatible parts and fixtures
  • Enclosures and instrument packaging
  • CAD models and manufacturing drawings

Ways to work together

Design review

A short engagement on a design you already have. Schematics, optical layouts or a noise budget are looked over and you get back a written assessment: what will limit performance, what is likely to cause trouble in build or test, and what to change first.

Subsystem design

A defined block — a detector front end, a control loop, a mount — taken from requirements through to tested hardware, with the design files and test data handed over so you can build more of them yourself.

Complete instrument

Specification to working hardware. Optics, electronics, firmware and mechanics designed as one system, delivered with documentation and a characterisation report against the agreed requirements.

Start a project

Send a short description of what you need to measure or control, along with any constraints you already know about — space, budget, timescale, environment. An initial conversation about scope and feasibility costs nothing.
