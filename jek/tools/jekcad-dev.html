      Variables

      Each variable is a name and an expression, which may use the ones above it. Use one in any field, or in a sketch’s dimension, as #name: change it here, and every feature that uses it follows.

      Mouse and keys

      Looking

      Right-drag
      orbit
      Middle-drag, or Shift and right-drag
      pan
      Wheel
      zoom about the pointer
      F, or double-click on nothing
      zoom to fit
      N
      look normal to the flat face, plane or sketch chosen
      1 to 7

      View cube

      Fly (the bird in the bar)

      Choosing

      Click
      a face, edge, vertex, sketch region or plane (the filter, top right, limits it)
      Click again
      each click adds to the selection; a click on what is chosen takes it out, a click on nothing empties it
      Drag from nothing
      select by a box
      Esc
      select nothing, or close the feature dialog
