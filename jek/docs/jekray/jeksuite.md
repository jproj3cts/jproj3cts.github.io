**JEKsuite** ([jeksys.net/jek/tools/jeksuite.html](/jek/tools/jeksuite.html)) is where the JEK apps and your cloud benches meet. It opens in a tab of its own: from an app, the cloud button beside the bench's name, **File › My benches on JEKsuite…**, or a link ending `#benches`. The same tab is reused each time, and so is each app's, so a bench chosen in JEKsuite opens in its app's tab rather than a new one.

## The apps

**New** in the bar starts a bench or a coating, opened in its app's own tab. Each app also has a page here, from the top of the left-hand list (see below). Signed out, the apps are under the sign-in panel. A new bench is a JEKray bench, a JEKion bench, or an optical or RF design (JEKray opens straight into that designer); **JEKfilm coating** starts a coating in [JEKfilm](/jek/docs/jekray/jekfilm/). With JEKrayPro, or in a team with a plan, the new bench is made in the cloud first, in the workspace and folder you are looking at (or another you choose), and opens in its app already saving there. Without, it starts in the app itself, kept in that browser, where **Save to cloud** can put it in the cloud later.

## Your benches

Down the left: the apps, each with its mark in its colour (JEKray yellow, JEKion light blue, JEKfilm black, JEKcad red); your workspaces (your own, and each team you are in), the one shown with its folders under it as a tree; then **Recent**, **My parts** (or a team's parts), **My links** and the **Bin**. Each folder in the tree says how many benches it holds, the folders inside it counted too, and a folder with folders in it opens with the ▸ beside it. The folders of the place shown are also in the page, each with a dot for every app that has benches in it, and in the path along the top.

Each bench is a card with its picture and a label for its app; one with no picture yet shows its app's mark. Click a card to open the bench in its app. Where benches of several apps are shown, a row of chips above them shows one app's alone. In **Recent** and in a search, each bench says which folder it is in, and the folder's name goes there.

The button beside the sort order turns the cards into a table, a column each for the name, the app, the folder, when it changed and its size. Click a column's heading to sort by it, and again to turn the order round.

### Moving, picking and renaming

- **Drag** a bench onto a folder to move it there: a folder's card, a folder in the tree, or a place in the path along the top. Drag it onto the **Bin** to delete it. A folder is dragged into another folder the same way.
- **Pick several** with the tick on each card (it shows under the pointer), by Ctrl-clicking, or by Shift-clicking for every bench from the last one picked to this one. Ctrl+A picks every bench shown, Escape lets go of them. A bar then has **Move to**, **Copy to** another workspace and **Delete** for all of them at once, and dragging one of them drags them all. The Delete key bins what is picked.
- **Rename** a bench or a folder where it is: press F2 with the keyboard on it, or choose **Rename** in its **⋯** menu, type the name and press Enter. Escape leaves the name as it was. **New folder** is named the same way.

A bench's **⋯** menu gives:

- **History**: its versions, newest first, with the forks made from it drawn as branches. Open a copy of any version, **Compare** two versions item by item, **Fork from here**, or **Restore** one. A fork's history has **Merge this fork into it** and **Bring its changes in**: the merge is worked out item by item, any clash chosen by hand, and saved as a new version of the bench it goes into, the version before kept to come back to.
- **Fork**, **Copy to** another workspace, **Rename**, **Move to** a folder, and **Delete** (to the Bin for 30 days, where **Restore** brings it back).

**My parts** lists the parts and assemblies you kept from JEKray, to rename or delete; they are added to a bench from JEKray's **+ Add part › ★ My parts**. **My links** lists the short share links you made, how often each was opened, and lets you copy or delete one.


## An app's page

Click **JEKray**, **JEKion** or **JEKfilm** at the top of the left-hand list for that app's page in JEKsuite, rather than the app itself; **Open JEKray** (or JEKion, or JEKfilm) there opens the app. The page says what the app does and has its buttons: **Open JEKray** (or JEKion, or JEKfilm), **New bench** (**New coating** for JEKfilm), and for JEKray **Optical design** and **RF design**. Under them are that app's benches alone, with the same folders, search, sorting and cards or rows as My benches; each folder says how many of the app's benches it holds. Beside them, one bench is shown larger, with where it is, when it last changed and by whom, its size and its version: **Open in JEKray** opens it, **History** shows its versions, and **⋯** has the rest. Click a bench to open it. The one shown beside the list is the one under the pointer, or the one the keyboard is on; before that, the one changed last.

Each app's page has its own address, `jeksuite.html#app=jekray`, `#app=jekion` or `#app=jekfilm`, so it can be bookmarked, and the browser's Back button comes back to it. Anything else in the left-hand list leaves it. Signed out, the page still says what the app does and opens it; its benches show once you sign in.

## Your account, plan and teams

The button at the top right signs you in (Google, Microsoft, or an email address with a link, a code or a password) and holds the account menu: your plan, **Your account** (your name, picture and password), **Billing and invoices**, switching between monthly and yearly billing, each of your **teams** (members, invitations, roles, the team's plan and billing, and the web domain it signs part packs with) and **Create a team**, a university's licence for those who look after it, linking another way to sign in, signing out, deleting the account, and the theme. One account signs you in to every app. **Get Pro** shows JEKrayPro's plans and prices.
