Version 1.3, 5 October 2026

These terms cover JEKrayPro (formerly called JEKray2D Pro), the paid subscription from JEK Systems that adds a cloud account to the JEKray2D optical bench simulator: saving benches online, version history and team workspaces. The simulator itself stays free and is covered by the JEKray2D Licence, which also applies when you use it with a JEKrayPro account. How we handle personal data is set out in our Privacy Policy. By creating an account or taking out a subscription you agree to these terms.

1. Your account

  • You sign in with a Google account or a Microsoft university or work account. Keep that account secure: anything done when signed in as you is treated as done by you.
  • Each account is for one person. Teams share work through a team workspace, not by sharing a sign-in.
  • You must be at least 13 to have an account, and subscriptions must be bought by an adult.

2. Plans and prices

  • Individual: £9.99 a month, or £99 a year.
  • Academic: £4.99 a month, or £49 a year, for current students and staff of a university or similar institution. You qualify through an email address at your institution, on your Google or Microsoft sign-in; if you have none, email us and we will check by hand. If you stop qualifying, your subscription moves to the Individual price at its next renewal, and we will tell you before it does.
  • Team: £9.99 per seat a month, with a minimum of 3 seats. Owners, admins and editors take a paid seat; viewers do not.
  • Institutional: by written agreement with JEK Systems. Contact sales@jeksys.net.

The price you pay, including any VAT that applies, is shown before you pay. We may change prices for future billing periods with at least 30 days’ notice by email; if you do not want to continue at the new price you can cancel before it applies.

3. Payment and renewal

  • Payments are taken by Stripe at the start of each billing period. Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each month or year until you cancel. For yearly plans we email you a reminder before each renewal.
  • Adding seats to a team is charged straight away for the rest of the current period. Removed seats come off at the next renewal.
  • If a payment fails, we will email you and Stripe will retry. Cloud saving keeps working for 7 days; after that it stops until payment is made, but you can still open and export your benches.
  • Invoices, card details and plan changes are managed in Billing, which opens Stripe’s secure customer portal.

The rest of the period is not refunded, except under the 14-day right below.

To use this right, cancel in Billing and email support@jeksys.net, or simply email us.

You can still sign in, open your benches to view them and export them, as files or all together, for as long as you keep your account; you cannot edit them or save changes to the cloud. The same applies to a team workspace whose subscription ends. Subscribing again restores full access straight away. Benches you have saved to your own device are not affected.
