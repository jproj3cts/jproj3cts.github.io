Cavity finesse

% %

The values filled in are an example: a 100 mm cavity with two 99% mirrors, which has an FSR of 1.5 GHz and a linewidth of about 4.8 MHz.

Input parameters:
R₁, R₂ = mirror power reflectivities (the cavity is taken to have no other loss)
FSR = free spectral range
Δν = full width at half maximum of one resonance
$$ \mathcal{F} = \frac{\pi \, (R_1 R_2)^{1/4}}{1 - \sqrt{R_1 R_2}} $$
$$ \mathcal{F} = \frac{\mathrm{FSR}}{\Delta \nu} $$
Finesse 𝓕 = —
Coefficient of finesse F = —
Output parameters:
𝓕 = finesse, the free spectral range divided by the linewidth (the reflectivity formula holds for a high finesse, roughly 𝓕 above 10)
F = coefficient of finesse, 4R/(1 − R)² with R = √(R₁R₂), the factor in the Airy transmission function; F = (2𝓕/π)². It is a different quantity from the finesse.

Formula: B. E. A. Saleh and M. C. Teich, Fundamentals of Photonics, 3rd ed. (Wiley, 2019).
