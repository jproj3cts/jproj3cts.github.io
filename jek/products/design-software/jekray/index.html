Waves, not just rays

Interference, cavities and polarisation

Each ray carries its polarisation and optical path, so interferometers show fringes and cavities resonate. A Gaussian beam is followed through every branch, and the diffracted field is worked out where the rays cannot tell the story: slits, masks, a tight focus, a vortex beam.

Real glass

The makers’ catalogues, built in

Schott, Ohara, Hoya, CDGM and Sumita, some 1,200 glasses by their own dispersion formulas, plus the crystals and optical plastics. Import a Thorlabs or Edmund lens file and it traces in the glass its maker chose; export a lens as a prescription your lens-design program opens.

Three dimensions

The diffracted field in 3D

Raise, tip and roll any part and the light follows, periscopes and misaligned cavities included. The field glows along each beam in calibrated W/cm², interference and all; point at it to read it. Bring mounts and housings in from CAD as STL files.

One bench

Fibre, free space and the signals between

Fibre subsystems, free-space optics and the electronics after the detector on one bench, across as many tabs as you like: photodiode signals, oscilloscopes, lock-ins and lock error signals. Spectra, ultrafast pulses, nonlinear optics, vapour cells and optical traps. And RF from 1 GHz to 3 THz: feed horns, RF lenses, mirrors, wire grids and mesh filters, with plastic lenses made to order that show their loss, how well the beam fits them, and whether they can be made.

An AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and others) can write JEKray projects for you. Give it the guide below, which teaches it the file format, the conventions and the checks to make, then ask for a .jekray file.

Download the JEKray guide for AI assistants (.md)

Some things to ask for:

  • “Design a Michelson interferometer at 1064 nm with 100 mm arms and a photodiode on the output. Give me a .jekray file.”
  • “Rebuild the optical setup in figure 1 of the attached paper as a .jekray file. Put your assumptions in the notes.”
  • “Add a mode-matching lens pair so this laser matches the cavity in the file I attached.”

Paste the answer into JEKray with Open ▾ → Pasted text (or Ctrl+V on the bench). If anything could not be used as written, the tool lists it: copy that report back to the assistant and it can fix the file. Check what it built against your source before relying on it.

Signing in is optional: the simulator works the same without an account, and keeps your work in your browser. With JEKrayPro, benches are stored by JEK Systems on Cloudflare, with their history; card payments are handled by Stripe, and we never see card numbers.

JEKrayPro Terms Privacy Policy Security overview Accessibility statement JEKrayPro for universities

JEKray works in the plane of the bench. It has no skew rays, no out-of-plane tilts and no anamorphic systems, and it does not model scatter, thermal effects or coating dispersion. Power figures ignore absorption unless you set it on a material.

The documentation sets out each model, its equations and its limits, element by element.

The validation page runs JEKray against published results and independent codes, live in your browser. For anything that gets built, funded or published, check the numbers in an established optical design package first.
