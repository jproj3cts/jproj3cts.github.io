Laser power density

The values filled in are an example: a 1 W continuous-wave laser with a beam of 0.5 mm radius.

Input parameters
P = Laser power (for a pulsed laser, the peak power gives the peak intensity in the pulse)
w = Gaussian beam radius where the intensity falls to 1/e² (13.5%) of its peak; half the 1/e² diameter
$$ I_0 = \frac{2 P}{\pi w^2} \qquad \bar I = \frac{P}{\pi w^2} $$

Average intensity Ī = —
Output parameters
I0 = Intensity on the axis of a Gaussian beam
Ī = Power divided by the area πw², half the peak; 1 W/cm² = 10⁴ W/m²

Formula: B. E. A. Saleh and M. C. Teich, Fundamentals of Photonics, 3rd ed. (Wiley, 2019).
